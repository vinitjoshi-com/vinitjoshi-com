# Vinit Joshi, DDS

### Founder · AI systems builder · Independent research lead

I bring a background in dentistry to building software and directing interdisciplinary research. My work spans **CurrenTDx**, an AI-assisted dental operations platform, and **Spin Quantum**, a research program exploring magnetic systems and probabilistic computing.

I connect domain problems to system requirements, direct AI-assisted implementation, and organize the evidence needed to decide what works and what to investigate next.

## CurrenTDx | Dental operations and decision support

Dental technology decisions connect equipment costs, procedure economics, workflow capacity, and the practical challenge of adopting a new process. My work brings those questions into a common product architecture.

**My contributions**

- Defined a systems approach connecting supply-side economics and demand-side operations.
- Developed a framework that works backward from desired operational outcomes to the information, decisions, and workflow support required.
- Mapped domain requirements into bounded agent responsibilities and directed AI-assisted implementation and integration.
- Specified review, traceability, context-sensitive output, and sensitive-information screening requirements.
- Funded and managed the supporting infrastructure, domains, and model access.

**Technical context:** Python, LangGraph, FastAPI, model APIs, and Linux service deployment. These describe the project's architecture and development environment; my contribution combines product leadership, systems design, and AI-assisted integration.

**Status:** documented architecture and deployed server components. Full workflow validation, clinical readiness, and measured business outcomes are separate milestones. The interdisciplinary framework is a design approach, not a guarantee of adoption or financial results.

## Spin Quantum | Modeling, research direction, and evidence

Spin Quantum explores magnetic-system behavior and its possible use in probabilistic computation. I direct research questions and coordinate a process that separates hypotheses, simulations, literature findings, and proposed physical experiments.

**My contributions**

- Framed research questions and requested controls that distinguish interactions between components from changes in individual behavior.
- Directed bounded modeling and review cycles with explicit assumptions and stopping conditions.
- Challenged source interpretations and requested newer evidence when existing literature did not settle a question.
- Coordinated reviews across AI providers, resolving disagreements against evidence rather than treating consensus as validation.
- Established dated records that distinguish human input, model assistance, executed analysis, and unresolved conclusions.
- Translated research gaps into technical briefs and proposed measurement work.

**Evidence:** project records document a reproducible simulation checkpoint with trajectory checks, statistical analysis, analyzer tests, and comparison with an equilibrium calculation. Later source audits retained unresolved conclusions where the evidence was insufficient.

**Status:** computational research and source evaluation. The work does not yet establish fabricated hardware, a working quantum computer, or commercial advantage.
